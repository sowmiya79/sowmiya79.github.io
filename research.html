Urban Proximity and the Double Burden of Malnutrition in Rural India

Solo-authored · To be presented at GSC 2027, Groningen

Using 214,787 rural mother–child pairs from the National Family Health Surveys (2015–16 and 2019–21), linked to each village's distance from the nearest town, I examine how maternal overweight, child stunting and their co-occurrence vary with urban proximity. The findings suggest that the double burden in rural India is better understood as a feature of places undergoing nutrition transition than of individual households.
